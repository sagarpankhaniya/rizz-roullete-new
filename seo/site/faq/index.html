What is Rizz Roulette?

Rizz Roulette is an adults-only random video chat platform that pairs you one to one with a stranger, and holds the best gender ratio in the category by balancing the matching queue itself, so you are not skipping through a long run of the same match. It runs in the browser, needs no download, and is free to start.

How is the gender ratio better than other sites?

Because we fix it at the queue instead of filtering what you see. Matching priority is allocated to keep both sides of the queue balanced, so the pool you are drawn from is already close to even before a match is made — and a paid creator programme keeps the thinner side populated. A filter hides people from you. This changes who is waiting.

Is Rizz Roulette free?

Yes, free to start with no account required. A premium tier and a coin system for tipping creators are optional, and the core matching experience does not sit behind them.

Do I need to sign up or download anything?

No. Confirm you are 18 or over, allow camera access, and you are matched. It runs in any modern browser on desktop or mobile.

What is the difference between the moderated and unmoderated rooms?

You choose at entry. The moderated room enforces a clothed, no-explicit-content policy and is actively monitored. The unmoderated room is adults-only and permits explicit content between consenting verified adults. Both require age verification and both ban for harassment, recording without consent, or any content involving a minor.

Is random video chat safe?

The honest answer is that it depends far more on your own behaviour than on the platform. Stay anonymous, never share your full name, location, socials or financial details, and skip immediately if a conversation feels wrong. On our side we run age verification, device fingerprinting to make ban evasion harder, and a reporting route on every match. Read our safety page for the full picture.

How do you get a better gender ratio than other sites?

Through matching priority plus a paid creator programme. Both sides of the queue are tracked continuously and priority goes to whichever side is under-represented, so the pool stays close to even. Nobody is hidden from anybody — the composition of the queue changes, not your view of it. The creator programme is what keeps the thinner side populated, which is the part most platforms never solve.

Does the ratio hold at every hour?

Not perfectly, and we would rather say so than claim otherwise. Traffic on both sides varies through the day and by region, so the ratio moves. It is a target the queue is actively steered toward rather than a fixed number we can promise, and the creator programme exists specifically to hold it during off-peak hours.

What is device fingerprinting used for?

Only to make ban evasion harder. If an account is banned for harassment or for content involving a minor, a device signal makes it more difficult to simply return with a new session. It is not used for advertising or sold to anyone.

What happens if age verification gets it wrong?

Automated age estimation produces false results in both directions, and an adult who is wrongly blocked can fall back to a document check. If that also fails, contact support@rizzroulette.chat and a human will review it.

Are chats recorded or stored?

Video streams are peer-to-peer and are not recorded by us. Recording another user without their consent is a permanent ban and is illegal in many jurisdictions.

How do I report someone?

Every match has a report control. Reports are reviewed, and reports involving a minor or non-consensual content are escalated immediately and, where required, referred to law enforcement.

What gets you banned?

Any content involving a minor, any non-consensual recording or sharing, harassment, threats, and attempting to move a user off-platform for a scam. Bans are tiered, and there is an appeal route for automated decisions.

I was banned and I think it was a mistake. What now?

Automated moderation produces false positives. Email support@rizzroulette.chat and a human will review the decision.

What does Rizz Roulette cost?

Free to start with no account. Optional paid elements are a premium tier and a coin system used for tipping creators. The core matching experience is not behind either.

Can I use Rizz Roulette on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on iOS and Android with no app install, which also means no app store content restrictions on the unmoderated room.

How do creators earn on Rizz Roulette?

Through tips, and creators keep 80%. Details are on the creator programme page.

Is Rizz Roulette available in my country?

It is available in most countries, but adult platform access is restricted by law in some jurisdictions and by age-verification requirements in others. Where access is restricted you will be told at entry rather than silently blocked.
