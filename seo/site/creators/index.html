How this differs from subscription platforms

On a subscription platform your income is a function of the audience you brought with you. Building that audience is most of the work, it happens off-platform, and it is why the earnings distribution on those sites is so lopsided — the people at the top arrived with a following and the people at the bottom are still trying to build one. If you are in the second group, working harder inside the platform does not fix it, because the constraint is external.

Random matching removes that constraint entirely. You are not competing for a place in anyone's feed and you are not ranked against creators with a five-year head start. Every session puts you in front of people who did not come looking for you specifically, which means discovery is the platform's job rather than yours.

The trade-off is real and worth stating: earnings here are per-session rather than recurring, so there is no monthly subscription base to rely on. Some creators run both — subscriptions for recurring income, random chat for reach and for the days the feed is quiet.

What we ask of you

Age and identity verification before your first payout, without exception. No content involving anyone under 18, ever, and no exceptions or appeals on that. No recording or sharing another user's stream without their consent — our safety and moderation policy applies to creators exactly as it does to everyone else. Beyond that, you set your own room, your own hours and your own boundaries, and you can end any session at any time without explaining why.

Questions

How much do creators keep?

80% of every tip. The remaining 20% covers processing, payouts and platform costs.

Do I need a following to join?

No. Matching is random, so traffic does not depend on an audience you bring with you. This is the main structural difference from subscription platforms, where earnings track the following you already had.

How do payouts work?

Payouts run to a supported payment provider on a regular schedule. Requirements including age and identity verification apply before the first payout.

Do I have to appear in the unmoderated room?

No. You choose your room, and the moderated room has a clothed, no-explicit-content policy. Tipping works in both.
