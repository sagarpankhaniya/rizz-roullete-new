About

Who builds this

Rizz Roulette is operated by Rizz Roulette LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company formed on 3 June 2026. The platform is built and run by a small founding team of three.

Why we built it

Random video chat has not meaningfully changed since 2009. The interfaces improved; the core problem did not. Queues stayed unbalanced and the industry converged on the same response — charge for a filter and let the underlying imbalance stand. We thought the queue itself was the thing to fix, and that fixing it required paying the side of the queue everyone else takes for granted. That is the whole thesis of the product.

What we will not do

Worth stating, because the category has a credibility problem and most of it is self-inflicted. We do not display fabricated online-user counts. We do not run bot accounts to pad a thin queue. We do not publish invented testimonials or star ratings. If you have used random chat platforms for any length of time you have encountered all three, and you probably assume everyone does it. We would rather compete on the one number that is real.

We also will not claim the ratio holds perfectly at every hour in every region, because it does not. It is a target the queue is actively steered toward, and the creator programme exists to hold it during the hours when that is hardest. Our how it works page covers the mechanism, including its limits.
