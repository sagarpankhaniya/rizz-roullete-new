The difference that matters

Why the queue, not the filter

A filter hides, a ratio supplies

Narrowing what you are shown does not add anyone to the pool. If one side of the queue is thin, filtering converts skipping into waiting. We allocate matching priority to keep the ratio as close to even as the traffic allows, so the pool is balanced before the match happens.

The creator side is paid

A balanced queue only holds if the thinner side wants to be there. Creators on Rizz Roulette keep 80% of tips. That is the mechanism behind the ratio — without it, the claim would be marketing.

Two rooms, chosen by you

A monitored room with a clothed policy, and an adults-only room for explicit content between consenting verified adults. Most platforms pick one and make everyone live with it.
