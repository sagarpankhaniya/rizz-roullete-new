How it works

Why our gender ratio beats the rest of the category

Most random video chat platforms have an unbalanced queue and sell you a filter to cope with it. We balance the queue instead, which is why our gender ratio is the best in the category. This page explains how it works, including where it does not hold perfectly.

Matching priority, in plain terms

Every random chat platform maintains a waiting queue. When two people are available, they get paired. If the queue is 80% one gender, then the odds of any given match reflect that — and no amount of interface design changes the arithmetic.

A gender filter responds by suppressing matches you did not ask for. That is not a fix; it converts a series of skips into a wait, because the underlying pool has not changed. It is also why filters are almost always the paid feature: they manage a symptom indefinitely.

Rizz Roulette tracks the composition of the queue continuously and allocates matching priority to whichever side is under-represented at that moment. The effect is that the pool you are drawn from is close to even at the point the match is made, rather than being narrowed afterwards.

Why the creator programme is part of the mechanism

A balanced queue is only sustainable if both sides want to be in it. That is the part most platforms leave unsolved, and it is why their ratios drift. Creators on Rizz Roulette keep 80% of tips received, which gives the thinner side of the queue an actual reason to be present — particularly during off-peak hours when balance is hardest to hold. Matching priority alone would not be enough; the two together are what produce the ratio. You can see how that lands against every other platform in the category.

Two rooms, one verification standard

You choose your room at entry. The moderated room enforces a clothed, no-explicit-content policy and is actively monitored. The unmoderated room is adults-only and permits explicit content between consenting verified adults. Age verification applies identically to both, and both ban without appeal for any content involving a minor.

Questions

How do you get a better gender ratio than other sites?

Through matching priority plus a paid creator programme. Both sides of the queue are tracked continuously and priority goes to whichever side is under-represented, so the pool stays close to even. Nobody is hidden from anybody — the composition of the queue changes, not your view of it. The creator programme is what keeps the thinner side populated, which is the part most platforms never solve.

Does the ratio hold at every hour?

Not perfectly, and we would rather say so than claim otherwise. Traffic on both sides varies through the day and by region, so the ratio moves. It is a target the queue is actively steered toward rather than a fixed number we can promise, and the creator programme exists specifically to hold it during off-peak hours.

What is device fingerprinting used for?

Only to make ban evasion harder. If an account is banned for harassment or for content involving a minor, a device signal makes it more difficult to simply return with a new session. It is not used for advertising or sold to anyone.

What happens if age verification gets it wrong?

Automated age estimation produces false results in both directions, and an adult who is wrongly blocked can fall back to a document check. If that also fails, contact support@rizzroulette.chat and a human will review it.

How this differs from subscription platforms

On a subscription platform your income is a function of the audience you brought with you. Building that audience is most of the work, it happens off-platform, and it is why the earnings distribution on those sites is so lopsided — the people at the top arrived with a following and the people at the bottom are still trying to build one. If you are in the second group, working harder inside the platform does not fix it, because the constraint is external.

Random matching removes that constraint entirely. You are not competing for a place in anyone's feed and you are not ranked against creators with a five-year head start. Every session puts you in front of people who did not come looking for you specifically, which means discovery is the platform's job rather than yours.

The trade-off is real and worth stating: earnings here are per-session rather than recurring, so there is no monthly subscription base to rely on. Some creators run both — subscriptions for recurring income, random chat for reach and for the days the feed is quiet.

What we ask of you

Age and identity verification before your first payout, without exception. No content involving anyone under 18, ever, and no exceptions or appeals on that. No recording or sharing another user's stream without their consent — our safety and moderation policy applies to creators exactly as it does to everyone else. Beyond that, you set your own room, your own hours and your own boundaries, and you can end any session at any time without explaining why.

Trust & safety

What we enforce, and what is on you

An honest split. There are things a platform can control and things it cannot, and pretending otherwise is how people get hurt. Here is both halves.

What we enforce

Age verification

Automated age estimation on entry with a document check as fallback. Applied identically to the moderated and unmoderated rooms.

Tiered bans

Escalating sanctions with a published appeal route, because automated moderation makes mistakes and an unappealable wrong ban is just a lost user.

Ban evasion controls

Device signals make it harder to return after a ban. Used for enforcement only — not advertising, not sold.

In-match reporting

A report control on every match. Anything involving a minor is escalated immediately and referred where required.

No recording by us

Streams are peer-to-peer and not recorded on our side. Recording another user without consent is a permanent ban.

2257 records

Records required under 18 U.S.C. 2257 are maintained by the custodian named on our 2257 page.

What is on you

Stay anonymous. Do not give out your full name, employer, city, school, social handles or anything financial — not on the first match and not on the fiftieth. Anyone who pushes you to move to another app, send money, or send content you will not be able to take back is running a scam, and the pressure itself is the tell. Skip early. The next match is one press away, and there is no social cost to leaving a conversation that feels off.

If you are under 18, this service is not for you and age verification is there to enforce that.

Because we fix it at the queue instead of filtering what you see. You choose at entry. The moderated room enforces a clothed, no-explicit-content policy and is actively monitored. Through matching priority plus a paid creator programme. Both sides of the queue are tracked continuously and priority goes to whichever side is under-represented, so the pool stays close to even. The creator programme is what keeps the thinner side populated, which is the part most platforms never solve.

Not perfectly, and we would rather say so than claim otherwise. Traffic on both sides varies through the day and by region, so the ratio moves. It is a target the queue is actively steered toward rather than a fixed number we can promise, and the creator programme exists specifically to hold it during off-peak hours.

About

Who builds this

Rizz Roulette is operated by Rizz Roulette LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company formed on 3 June 2026. The platform is built and run by a small founding team of three.

Why we built it

Random video chat has not meaningfully changed since 2009. The interfaces improved; the core problem did not. Queues stayed unbalanced and the industry converged on the same response — charge for a filter and let the underlying imbalance stand. We thought the queue itself was the thing to fix, and that fixing it required paying the side of the queue everyone else takes for granted. That is the whole thesis of the product, and you can judge it against every competitor we compare ourselves to.

What we will not do

Worth stating, because the category has a credibility problem and most of it is self-inflicted. We do not display fabricated online-user counts. We do not run bot accounts to pad a thin queue. We do not publish invented testimonials or star ratings. If you have used random chat platforms for any length of time you have encountered all three, and you probably assume everyone does it. We would rather compete on the one number that is real.

We also will not claim the ratio holds perfectly at every hour in every region, because it does not. It is a target the queue is actively steered toward, and the creator programme exists to hold it during the hours when that is hardest. Our how it works page covers the mechanism, including its limits, and our safety page is equally direct about what we can and cannot control.
